# Part Three — Have One

## Episode architecture

- **Owner:** Deshpande
- **Governing question:** Is this illness, communication, or consent?
- **Concrete want:** Produce trustworthy longitudinal evidence while protecting Jenn and Arlo from being reduced to contaminated subjects.
- **Private need:** Accompany Jenn and retain ownership of the discovery rather than admit that both are already beyond his authority.
- **Irreversible choice:** Deshpande accepts a documented voluntary exposure, then—after convergence—supervises Mel's disclosed exposure.
- **Cost bearers:** Mel and Sofia; Deshpande's choice also validates the network's method.
- **Episode clock:** The four days from Jenn and Arlo's first stable echo through Mel's decision.
- **Final image:** Mel opens the labelled specimen bag and eats the doughnut.

## Scene outline

### 1. Better at the wrong jobs

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande; cold open.
- **Place / time:** Restricted laboratory wing, morning after Part Two.
- **First image:** Jenn completes a tray inventory from behind observation glass while Arlo, on another floor, reaches for each missing item at the same moment.
- **Want and process:** Deshpande audits their reports after Jenn discloses the delayed correlation. He separates shared observations from guesses and demands independent timestamps.
- **Turn / choice:** The two hosts identify small errors faster together than the uninfected staff, but Jenn admits she concealed the first stable echo.
- **Residue:** Her delay becomes an institutional breach and a uniquely valuable dataset. Deshpande keeps the study local rather than immediately surrender both hosts to separate federal sites.
- **Last image / next cause:** He writes **COOPERATION MAY ALTER PHENOMENON** and realizes observation itself now changes risk.

### 2. Toothpaste and the red line

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande observing Jenn and Arlo, who remain individual.
- **Place / time:** Paired interview rooms, day one.
- **First image:** Identical breakfast trays sit untouched while Jenn tastes mint from Arlo's toothpaste.
- **Want and process:** The team maps each echo to its likely sensory, autonomic, mnemonic, or semantic mode and requires verifiable sources.
- **Turn / choice:** Arlo recalls Jenn's humiliating first-day correction at the animal-room red line, a memory he could know only through her. The memory returns without his judgment attached.
- **Residue:** Shame becomes recognition rather than leverage; their fear decreases when they attend, making relief both evidence and biological pressure.
- **Last image / next cause:** Jenn looks at the red line through two closed doors and asks Deshpande to stop calling the effect noise.

### 3. The photograph

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande.
- **Place / time:** Interview room, day one.
- **First image:** Arlo's locked phone rests face down between Deshpande and Jenn.
- **Want and process:** Deshpande demands a demonstration with a traceable knowledge route. Jenn closes her eyes and describes an unpublished photograph that Arlo remembers clearly.
- **Turn / choice:** Arlo unlocks the phone himself and verifies the image; the network has accessed his memory, not the device.
- **Residue:** Ordinary explanations narrow. Jenn gives the full known risks, including her delayed report and the possibility that attending changes desire.
- **Last image / next cause:** Deshpande turns the phone face down again, but can no longer make the knowledge disappear.

### 4. The marked cup

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande.
- **Place / time:** Clinical observation suite, late day one.
- **First image:** Two cups sit on a tray, one marked with a red band and one unmarked.
- **Want and process:** Deshpande records his health, cognition, the broad exposure route, the known progression, and the limits of current knowledge so later changes can be measured against his unaltered judgment. Preparation and quantity remain unstated.
- **Plausible exit:** He can preserve scientific independence, transfer Jenn and Arlo, and study them without exposing himself.
- **Turn / choice:** He chooses the marked cup because a longitudinal observer inside the phenomenon may be the only way to distinguish communication from impairment—and because he refuses to leave Jenn alone in work he authorized.
- **Residue:** His active assent is documented alongside the warning that Stage 2+ judgment may change.
- **Last image / next cause:** He drinks, sets the empty marked cup beside his baseline form, and starts the timer himself.

### 5. The first repetition

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande.
- **Place / time:** Testing suite, days one and two.
- **First image:** A 78-second timer repeatedly reaches zero while three pens mark separate logs.
- **Want and process:** The trio tests attention, sensory load, distance, and timing without attempting to optimize transmission or manufacture. Each test has a baseline, a single change, and a stop condition.
- **Turn / choice:** Attention strengthens conscious access while overload weakens it. Their separate logs agree at narrow recurring intervals.
- **Residue:** A legible pattern replaces anecdote, but every successful test teaches the hosts how to cooperate more effectively.
- **Last image / next cause:** Deshpande knows which card Jenn will turn before her hand moves.

### 6. The cages that do nothing

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande.
- **Place / time:** Faraday room, jammer test bay, and shielded imaging room, day two.
- **First image:** A radio-spectrum trace goes flat while Jenn correctly records Arlo's distant sensory prompt.
- **Want and process:** The team eliminates ordinary electromagnetic paths, then transfers Arlo across the state and repeats the correlation test.
- **Turn / choice:** Shielding, jamming, and geographic distance do not alter the timing. Deshpande refuses Mara's language of transmission and writes that the brains appear to participate in the same state.
- **Residue:** No central signal source or ordinary channel remains to attack; the conceptual transceiver model gains its first human evidence.
- **Last image / next cause:** Mara circles the word **participate**, then slides over the first antisense culture result.

### 7. A limit before a cure

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande.
- **Place / time:** Diagnostic lab, day two.
- **First image:** Two conceptual graphs diverge: new S-78-complex signal falls in one sample while an established signal remains.
- **Want and process:** Mara explains the observed result at a high level: one antisense candidate suppresses new complex manufacture but does not dismantle complexes already present. No target, design, dose, delivery, culture condition, or formulation appears.
- **Turn / choice:** Deshpande is already beyond the uncertain early window. He declines a desperate unvalidated intervention and orders the candidate preserved and studied.
- **Residue:** The intervention gains an established limitation before it can become a finale solution.
- **Last image / next cause:** Mara labels the result **PREVENTION IS NOT REVERSAL** as Deshpande shivers once.

### 8. What anesthesia changes

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande.
- **Place / time:** Clinical imaging room, night two.
- **First image:** Arlo's EEG quiets under medically necessary deep anesthesia while Jenn's borrowed sensation of him thins.
- **Want and process:** During a necessary procedure, the team observes how sleep and anesthesia affect contribution without using them as a coercive experiment.
- **Turn / choice:** Conscious access vanishes with brain activity and returns during emergence; the field is intact, but the brain remains the processor.
- **Residue:** The finding rules out a mind stored elsewhere and plants the later controlled anesthesia interview as a limited tool, not a network blocker.
- **Last image / next cause:** Arlo wakes with Jenn's name in his mouth before he recognizes the room.

### 9. The useful answer

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande.
- **Place / time:** Staff corridor and clinic, day three.
- **First image:** A staff member drops a clipboard, then insists the numbness in one hand is fatigue.
- **Want and process:** Deshpande tries to remain inside protocol while a source-less diagnostic pattern from a connected veterinary pathologist presses into awareness.
- **Turn / choice:** He acts on the warning, calls a clinical emergency, and the staff member receives timely treatment. The overseas knowledge route is later traced through a progressing host connected to Jenn and Arlo.
- **Immediate success:** Pooled pattern recognition saves a named colleague without granting Deshpande borrowed hand skill or omniscience.
- **Residue:** The network's first honest sales pitch is a life saved through competence.
- **Last image / next cause:** The staff member's wedding ring is removed before swelling starts; Deshpande watches a stranger's knowledge become care.

### 10. Jenn's forty minutes

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande; Jenn remains observable but private access ends at convergence.
- **Place / time:** Orientation-capable clinical room, day three.
- **First image:** Jenn has taped her home address to the ceiling above the bed.
- **Want and process:** Deshpande follows the protocol Jenn helped write: clear the area, protect breathing and circulation, verify identity prompts, track the 78-second pattern, and ask before each comfort measure.
- **Turn / choice:** Her Stage 4 transition lasts forty minutes. She vomits, weeps, fights to repeat the address, and loses ownership of memories in waves.
- **Residue:** Convergence is a dangerous process rather than a shiver; the protocol saves her body but cannot preserve private viewpoint.
- **Last image / next cause:** Exhausted Jenn looks at Deshpande with recognition that belongs to more than one person.

### 11. Orientation

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande; Jenn is viewed externally as fully Joined.
- **Place / time:** Same room after stabilization, day three.
- **First image:** Jenn asks permission before removing the home-address card from the ceiling.
- **Want and process:** Deshpande tests whether the person before him is deceptive, impaired, or continuous. Jenn identifies what Stage 4 cost, what remains uncertain, and why the collective does not seek reversal.
- **Turn / choice:** Directly asked whether his present consent can remain unaltered after exposure, Jenn says no. The truthful limit makes her more credible, not less.
- **Plausible exit:** Deshpande can now withdraw, report, and preserve the boundary Jenn no longer possesses.
- **Residue:** He instead completes his progression protocol and chooses conscious convergence, convinced that accompaniment and evidence justify continuing.
- **Last image / next cause:** He hands Mara his last private observation sheet before lying down.

### 12. Deshpande's last private hour

- **POV / alignment:** Deshpande until convergence completes; never afterward.
- **Place / time:** Clinical room, day four.
- **First image:** His own baseline answers are read back to him in his own handwriting.
- **Want and process:** He follows Jenn's protocol, attending deliberately while naming which memories still feel singular.
- **Turn / choice:** Private humiliations arrive among other lives and are contextualized before he can defend them. The effect is not euphoria but the removal of the labour required to be known.
- **Residue / recognition:** Deshpande understands too late that his scientific ownership depended on a boundary he is choosing to surrender.
- **Last image / next cause:** His pen stops halfway through the word **I**. From this point, his body is seen only externally.

### 13. Sofia's name

- **POV / alignment:** Mel, with Joined Jenn and Deshpande external.
- **Place / time:** Laboratory break room, later day four.
- **First image:** The remaining fundraiser box has Sofia's trombone drawn on every label.
- **Want and process:** Jenn gives Mel proactive disclosure: broad route, stages, medical risks, altered-consent problem, and the current promise to wait if he refuses. She demonstrates pooled knowledge through Mel's previously witnessed panic, not a fact stolen from an uninfected mind.
- **Turn / choice:** Mel asks whether his assent also places Sofia in the experiment. Jenn says it belongs only to him and records Sofia as no-contact, while admitting the present collective cannot bind a future one.
- **Residue:** Sofia's refusal category becomes a moral promise the peaceful network will keep and acceleration will later break.
- **Last image / next cause:** Jenn prepares one doughnut as the vehicle for Mel's disclosed choice and seals it in a labelled specimen bag; method and quantity remain outside the frame.

### 14. Have one

- **POV / alignment:** Mel.
- **Place / time:** Break room, continuous.
- **First image:** The sealed doughnut sits between Mel's security badge and the no-contact notation bearing Sofia's name.
- **Want and process:** Mel asks Jenn to recount the worst part rather than the promised benefits. She describes Jenn's convergence accurately and does not claim certainty about continuity or future policy.
- **Turn / choice:** Given a clean opportunity to leave, Mel chooses exposure for himself because the saved staff member and the possibility of shared care outweigh the risk he now understands.
- **Immediate success:** Orientation secures active assent without trickery.
- **Residue / recognition:** Deshpande's choice has become a repeatable care protocol, and Mel's love has created a promise the growing collective may be unable to honour.
- **Final image:** Mel looks at the trombone on the label, opens the specimen bag, and eats the doughnut.

## Episode gate check

- **Two visible exits closed:** Deshpande can remain an uninfected observer before exposure; after Jenn's truthful orientation he can still report and withdraw.
- **Legible procedure:** Correlation testing, shielding and distance controls, clinically observed anesthesia, Stage 4 support, and informed orientation.
- **Choice creates turn:** Deshpande knowingly enters the phenomenon; after convergence, his authority and Jenn's care make Mel's choice credible.
- **Continuing residue:** First orientation protocol; antisense's prevention/reversal limit; shielding failure; Sofia's no-contact record; a saved colleague as moral evidence.
- **Rules audit:** Knowledge routes are explicit; brain activity changes participation; distance and shielding do not; no fully Joined private viewpoint; peaceful exposure uses disclosed risk and active assent.
- **Success and loss in final image:** Mel exercises informed choice, but the institution has learned how to make surrender feel responsibly supervised.
