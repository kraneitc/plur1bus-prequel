# Pluribus: Before We Were Us

A fan-created prequel project exploring the fourteen months between discovery of the extraterrestrial signal and humanity’s global Joining.

This project is unofficial and unaffiliated with Apple, Sony Pictures Television, High Bridge Productions, or the creators of *Pluribus*.

## Creative target

The intended final form is a six-part cinematic prose season: an episodic science-fiction novella with the narrative momentum and visual discipline of television, but enough interiority to portray the gradual experience of becoming aware of the host network.

The work may use established characters and premises from *Pluribus* through Season 1, Episode 4. It must not incorporate, research, imply, or spoil revelations from Episode 5 onward unless the user later changes that boundary.

The prose should be original. It may pursue high-level qualities associated with the series—patient observation, procedural credibility, dry humor, moral consequence, and sincere characters making defensible choices—but must not attempt to reproduce a particular living writer’s exact voice.

## Development workflow

The project should proceed through explicit gates rather than drafting the whole season at once.

### 1. Freeze the creative rules

Review and approve:

- infection stages and timing;
- transmission routes and dose logic;
- mystical telemetry and its limitations;
- Joined knowledge and truthfulness;
- the consent problem;
- viewpoint architecture;
- the policy for overlap with Episode 1.

Rule changes must be recorded in progress.md and reconciled with the treatment and season ledger before new prose is written.

### 2. Break all six episodes

Create scene-level episode outlines before writing further chapters. Each episode needs:

- one governing viewpoint character;
- one concrete want;
- one irreversible choice;
- at least one legible process sequence;
- a named person who absorbs the cost;
- residue that affects a later episode;
- a final image that changes the meaning of the choice.

### 3. Write one representative sample

The preferred calibration sample is Jenn’s first forty-seven hours after the rat bite. It should test:

- close-third prose and interiority;
- procedural and scientific density;
- the pace of infection;
- dry humor;
- the phenomenology of network awareness;
- whether persuasion feels attractive without becoming benign.

### 4. Revise the sample with the user

Do not extrapolate one unreviewed sample into an entire season. Use user feedback to create a stable prose standard first.

### 5. Draft one episode at a time

For each episode:

1. approve the scene outline;
2. write a first draft;
3. collect user notes;
4. perform one focused revision;
5. update the ledgers and progress tracker;
6. obtain approval before advancing.

### 6. Complete season-wide revisions

After all six episodes exist, audit:

- elapsed time, incubation, contagious periods, and travel;
- character knowledge and information access;
- telemetry and shielding rules;
- truth and consent behavior;
- recurring objects and procedural residue;
- named casualties and continuing emotional cost;
- disappearance of private viewpoint after convergence;
- spoiler compliance through S01E04.
