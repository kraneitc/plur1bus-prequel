# Part Five — For Your Own Good

## Episode architecture

- **Owner:** Lieutenant Colonel Evelyn Rios
- **Governing question:** Which intervention causes less harm?
- **Concrete want:** Interrupt the network's coordinated capability without producing the infrastructure collapse Davis predicted.
- **Private need:** Prove that restraint is not surrender and that a correct threat classification still permits a clean protective action.
- **Irreversible choice:** Rios executes simultaneous containment and cuts coordinated communications and transport; the operation succeeds locally for eleven minutes and helps provoke acceleration.
- **Cost bearer:** The global public. Omar, Tom, Mara, and Sofia make that cost personal before convergence begins.
- **Episode clock:** The first four days after containment starts.
- **Final image:** Aircraft take parallel headings while unmarked packages move below as ordinary cargo.

## Scene outline

### 1. Eleven minutes

- **POV / alignment:** Rios; cold open.
- **Place / time:** Mobile containment command, dawn.
- **First image:** Dozens of body-camera tiles show Joined people sitting down before tactical teams cross the threshold.
- **Want and process:** Rios runs the simultaneous-isolation doctrine: site confirmation, medical separation, local communication cuts, evidence preservation, and scheduled restoration checks.
- **Turn / choice:** No site resists. The operation reaches every known node and holds them apart for eleven minutes, exactly as designed.
- **Immediate success:** Coordinated movements stop at the targeted facilities.
- **Residue:** Success supplies Rios with apparent proof that containment works while the calm detainees make force look gratuitous.
- **Last image / next cause:** At eleven minutes, a detainee in every camera tile turns toward the nearest command speaker.

### 2. Every name has a source

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Raid sites and command, continuous.
- **First image:** A Joined receptionist addresses an undercover officer by his childhood nickname.
- **Want and process:** Rios tests whether the network is bluffing or omniscient. Analysts trace the nickname to a connected academy roommate; other officer names come from a compromised duty roster and ordinary badge-office access.
- **Turn / choice:** The detainees identify every embedded officer and warn, truthfully, that frightened interruption will cause avoidable harm.
- **Residue:** The network's knowledge is invasive but bounded. Capturing connected personnel has made more of Rios's plan ordinarily accessible.
- **Last image / next cause:** A source annotation appears beside every destabilizing fact; none makes the facts less destabilizing.

### 3. Cut the lines

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Mobile command, minutes later.
- **First image:** Davis's muted infrastructure slide glows behind a live map of coordinated detainee speech.
- **Want and process:** Rios needs to prevent compromised personnel from executing a wider operation. Her staff can cut known telecommunications and pause selected transport links, though prior tests show this cannot block telemetry.
- **Turn / choice:** A medical adviser asks for a negotiated pause. Rios hears the detainees' concern as coercive framing and orders the cuts before the next synchronized interval.
- **Plausible exit:** She can keep physical custody while leaving civilian coordination intact and opening a supervised channel.
- **Residue:** Ordinary systems fragment; the nonlocal network does not. Rios has applied the value she claims to protect by restricting everyone else's choices.
- **Last image / next cause:** The communications map goes dark while the detainees continue answering one another without delay.

### 4. The answer arrives everywhere

- **POV / alignment:** Rios observing; fully Joined people have no private viewpoint.
- **Place / time:** Raid sites, clinics, homes, and transport offices, minutes later.
- **First image:** Unconnected clocks reach the same 78-second mark as Joined bodies stop separate tasks.
- **Want and process:** Rios asks Jenn and Deshpande what the collective has decided. They state the competing harms: continued slow orientation under escalating raids versus rapid exposure with coordinated support. They state uncertainty and projected deaths without softening either.
- **Turn / choice:** They say the collective will accelerate because it predicts fewer deaths from one supported transition than from repeated conflict and fragmented infrastructure.
- **Residue:** The peaceful rule of individual assent is explicitly abandoned. Sincerity, arithmetic, and truth do not make the decision consensual.
- **Last image / next cause:** A Stage 2 detainee says "no" to acceleration; the Joined answer that the decision no longer belongs to one body.

### 5. Harmless alone

- **POV / alignment:** Rios through incident feeds; Joined actions remain external.
- **Place / time:** Civilian systems across the day.
- **First image:** A baggage routing tag is changed by one authorized hand.
- **Want and process:** Rios's analysts try to distinguish ordinary work from coordinated exposure activity. They observe a pharmacist opening legitimate stock, a food-safety manager releasing an approved shipment, and a flight crew filing a legal plan.
- **Turn / choice:** Each act is harmless in isolation and traceable to an authorized worker. Together their timing shows a global operation already distributed beyond known nodes.
- **Residue:** There is no central command or single route to disable; contents, preparation, quantities, equipment changes, and optimization stay outside the frame.
- **Last image / next cause:** The analysts run out of red markers before they run out of ordinary systems.

### 6. Omar's correction

- **POV / alignment:** Rios via airport medical and maintenance feeds; Omar is progressing and remains individually accountable for his earlier report.
- **Place / time:** Airport operations centre, afternoon.
- **First image:** Omar places his original maintenance warning beside a new plan whose danger only his domain knowledge reveals.
- **Want and process:** He wants to prevent two terminals from receiving a materially more dangerous exposure than the collective intends. He reports the discrepancy without describing the calculation or equipment.
- **Turn / choice:** Connected staff accept his correction immediately, reducing predicted casualties while preserving the larger operation.
- **Residue:** Omar's competence saves people and enables acceleration; his accidental exposure has become institutional leverage for the network.
- **Last image / next cause:** Two terminal warning lights clear while the airport remains inside the broader pattern.

### 7. The curve was already there

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Military epidemiology cell, evening.
- **First image:** A sudden vertical outbreak curve lies over travel records that slope quietly backward for weeks.
- **Want and process:** Epidemiologists reconstruct broad progression windows from known convergence dates and ordinary movement records.
- **Turn / choice:** They prove the apparent explosion is a detection artifact: presymptomatic people have carried the phenomenon ahead of visible symptoms.
- **Residue:** The discovery validates the biology and destroys the premise that known sites define the network's edge.
- **Last image / next cause:** The containment perimeter expands beyond the border of the wall map.

### 8. Every prisoner is a briefing

- **POV / alignment:** Mara.
- **Place / time:** Rios's command post, night.
- **First image:** Mara lays Jenn's original two-host timing beside Rios's compromised site list.
- **Want and process:** Mara wants Rios to stop feeding the network access through captured hosts. She distinguishes continuous shared access from imitation and traces specific plan knowledge through connected personnel and records.
- **Turn / choice:** Rios realizes that her own command is progressively legible to the thing she is containing.
- **Residue:** Mara and Rios briefly share the same diagnosis but not the same definition of care.
- **Last image / next cause:** Rios sees a classified movement order correctly anticipated on a civilian traffic camera.

### 9. Stand down

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Command post, late night.
- **First image:** Rios's simultaneous-isolation order remains pinned above an empty restoration checklist.
- **Want and process:** She attempts to restore the slow, nonlethal status quo: halt raids, reopen lines, release medically stable detainees, and publish a supervised contact channel.
- **Turn / choice:** The Joined comply locally but say they will not stop acceleration. The present stand-down cannot guarantee that the next government response will remain restrained.
- **Plausible exit:** The order would have been a viable off-ramp before Rios demonstrated simultaneous global reach.
- **Residue / recognition:** She understands that her correct doctrine succeeded long enough to make future restraint unbelievable.
- **Last image / next cause:** Every raid clock is stopped at eleven minutes, but civilian departure boards keep changing.

### 10. No bargain

- **POV / alignment:** Mara; Joined Deshpande external.
- **Place / time:** Empty government cafeteria, day two.
- **First image:** Deshpande has placed the projected casualty model beside an untouched cup of coffee.
- **Want and process:** Mara uses the truth rule as leverage: she asks whether voluntary exposure of the original team would make the network stop.
- **Turn / choice:** Deshpande says no. He gives predicted exposures, failed convergences, interrupted-care deaths, and uncertainty without rounding down or claiming control over every outcome.
- **Residue:** Orientation is no longer a bargain. Mara sees that the care protocol she helped document has become a justification for conquest.
- **Last image / next cause:** Deshpande apologizes for pain the collective is choosing to impose, and the coffee remains untouched.

### 11. An ordinary grocery order

- **POV / alignment:** Mara intercut with external views of Joined Tom and a grocery worker.
- **Place / time:** Mara's safe house and an ordinary fulfilment centre, day two.
- **First image:** Mara annotates an olive substitution on a grocery order, a habit established across her marriage.
- **Want and process:** She needs food without revealing the safe house to official systems. A Joined grocery worker flags the obscure brand; Tom recognizes the annotation pattern through ordinary access to the order.
- **Turn / choice:** Under the new acceleration policy, contaminated prepared food in the delivery becomes the route to Mara. Preparation, amount, and handling remain unseen and unspecified.
- **Residue:** Tom's intimate care identifies her; the collective's policy, not secret malice, converts that knowledge into coercion. Exposure occurs days before symptoms.
- **Last image / next cause:** Mara throws away the olives but eats part of the prepared meal beside them.

### 12. One desperate use

- **POV / alignment:** Mara.
- **Place / time:** Original project laboratory, day three.
- **First image:** The entire remaining antisense research stock fits inside one locked cold-storage slot.
- **Want and process:** Mara reviews the observed early-window limit and stages the remaining stock for one desperate use. Target, dose, delivery, and formulation remain unstated.
- **Turn / choice:** She cannot justify spending the scarce material on an unknown mass of hosts and preserves it as evidence and a last individual intervention.
- **Residue:** The finale tool is physically placed and ethically burdened before Mara knows she needs it herself.
- **Last image / next cause:** She attaches a plain-language note: **STOPS NEW BUILD. DOES NOT REMOVE WHAT EXISTS.**

### 13. Jenn's hand

- **POV / alignment:** Mara; Joined Jenn external.
- **Place / time:** Original sample vault, day four.
- **First image:** Mara's hand holds the destruction control above the same sample inventory authorized in Part One.
- **Want and process:** She attempts to destroy every remaining original sample, knowing this will not damage a network with no central hub but hoping to remove one material source.
- **Turn / choice:** Jenn places her hand beneath the descending mechanism without threatening Mara. Mara can complete the process only by crushing it.
- **Choice under pressure:** Mara releases the control because she cannot intentionally maim Jenn, even after deciding Jenn is no longer privately herself.
- **Residue:** The samples remain, Mara's moral limit becomes usable knowledge, and the intervention stock remains locked nearby.
- **Last image / next cause:** Jenn withdraws an uninjured hand; Mara's own fingers begin a brief tremor she mistakes for adrenaline.

### 14. Parallel headings

- **POV / alignment:** Rios and Mara through separate ordinary views.
- **Place / time:** Airports, roads, distribution hubs, dusk of day four.
- **First image:** Aircraft on different continents begin turning onto parallel headings.
- **Want and process:** Rios tries to ground flights already operating within legal plans while Mara tracks ordinary cargo she cannot inspect in time.
- **Turn / choice:** There is no single object, aircraft, or command whose destruction ends the operation. Rios refuses indiscriminate force; that humane restraint cannot restore assent.
- **Immediate success and loss:** The Joined move support and exposure through functioning infrastructure rather than destroying it; humanity remains materially intact as private choice is overrun.
- **Final image:** Aircraft align overhead while unmarked packages travel below as ordinary cargo. Their contents and operation are never shown.

## Episode gate check

- **Two visible exits closed:** Rios rejects a negotiated pause before cutting lines; her later stand-down is a viable idea delivered after her success has closed it.
- **Legible procedure:** Simultaneous containment, source tracing, progression reconstruction, stand-down, and sample-destruction attempt.
- **Choice creates turn:** Rios's competent doctrine works locally and supplies the collective's stated reason to abandon assent.
- **Continuing residue:** Omar's correction; Mara's safe-house exposure through Tom; staged antisense stock; preserved original samples; Sofia's no-contact category now void.
- **Rules audit:** No central hub, no magical spread, no operational exposure detail, traceable knowledge routes, no Joined private viewpoint, and acceleration explicitly marks the consent breach.
- **Success and loss in final image:** Infrastructure works elegantly enough to carry away the public's last meaningful chance to refuse.
