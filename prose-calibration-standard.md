## Governing register

Use cinematic close-third prose with physical work in the foreground. Interior thought should arise from what the viewpoint character handles, notices, avoids, or misclassifies. Do not suspend the scene for thematic explanation.

The prose may be dryly funny, but humour comes from people using inadequate systems with complete professional seriousness. It must not release pressure or turn a character into a joke.

## Scene movement

1. Enter on an object, task, spatial problem, or interruption.
2. Establish what the viewpoint character is trying to complete.
3. Make the procedure legible through ordered physical action.
4. Change one element of the pattern.
5. Let a capable character notice and respond.
6. End after knowledge, leverage, risk, relationship, or moral position changes.

Time markers belong where elapsed time alters the medical or moral meaning. Do not label every interval merely because a clock exists.

## Sentence and paragraph rhythm

- Use short declarative sentences when a procedure narrows choice or a physical fact lands.
- Let longer sentences carry institutional accumulation, qualification, or a viewpoint character reasoning around something unwelcome.
- Use fragments only when they reproduce a professional checklist, involuntary perception, or genuine cognitive disruption.
- Preserve purposeful asymmetry. Dialogue, action paragraphs, and interior passages should not settle into one repeated length.
- Avoid repeated aphorisms, polished oppositions, and explanatory closing sentences. One concrete image should carry the implication.

## Dialogue

- Jenn solves the next practical problem, deflects loss of agency, and jokes to protect dignity.
- Deshpande corrects language because classification is how he cares and controls.
- Mara qualifies until evidence permits a narrower claim; pressure makes her more exact, not more eloquent.
- Arlo objects when institutional wording erases what happened to him. His speech is plain and sparing.
- Minor professionals speak from the authority and limits of their form, shift, room, or procedure.

No character explains information both speakers already know. Enter dialogue after pleasantries and leave when leverage changes.

## Scientific and safety abstraction

- Render purpose, observation order, stop conditions, symptoms, broad route, and result.
- Omit construction and cultivation workflow, sequences, targets, quantities, doses, environmental thresholds, apparatus modifications, optimization, intervention delivery, and formulation.
- Let credibility come from disciplined uncertainty and correct response to evidence.
- S-78 remains the single impossible premise. Institutions, injuries, time, and human attention remain ordinary.

## Infection and network perception

- Infection develops over days. An acute tremor is an event, not convergence.
- Early echoes arrive as specific sensory, autonomic, mnemonic, or semantic intrusions appropriate to progression.
- Every borrowed fact needs a connected source. The prose may delay naming that source, but the outline and continuity ledger must contain it.
- Do not format echoes as a generic chorus or disembodied voice. Make them resemble knowledge, sensation, memory, or emotion without ownership.
- Attention may stabilize access and provide real relief. That benefit must remain inseparable from its effect on later consent.
- After full convergence, the body leaves private viewpoint permanently.

## Procedure standard

On first appearance, a procedure needs:

- a visible goal;
- an ordered set of actions;
- a constraint or stop condition;
- a meaningful unknown;
- a deviation somebody notices;
- residue after the result.

Later repetitions should compress established steps unless the deviation changes risk or meaning.

## Moral and visual residue

The decisive turn must be a choice made for an understandable reason. An accidental exposure may create pressure, but it cannot perform the moral work.

Every major sequence leaves at least one persistent object or record: a damaged glove, separate incident systems, an unsigned transfer form, an observation note, a no-contact designation. Reuse should change the object's meaning rather than merely remind the reader it exists.

End each part on a physical image containing local success and continuing loss. Do not append an explanation of what the image means.
