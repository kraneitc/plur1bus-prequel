# Part Four — No One Is Sick

## Episode architecture

- **Owner:** Lieutenant Colonel Evelyn Rios
- **Governing question:** Can healthy people constitute an outbreak?
- **Concrete want:** Establish whether synchronized personnel form an adversarial biological network and obtain authority before it penetrates more defense systems.
- **Private need:** Keep threats categorizable so the doctrine she built—and the sacrifices it cost—remain useful.
- **Irreversible choice:** Rios classifies the Joined as an adversarial biological network and wins simultaneous-containment authority.
- **Cost bearers:** Stage 2–3 hosts, including the accidentally exposed airman and Omar; her family absorbs the relational cost.
- **Episode clock:** The last forty-eight hours of the peaceful first month.
- **Final image:** Joined people at separate raid locations look up together: "They found us."

## Scene outline

### 1. Intake without illness

- **POV / alignment:** Sofia, uninfected; cold open.
- **Place / time:** A discreet orientation centre, morning, one month after Patient Zero.
- **First image:** A receptionist erases **PATIENT** from a whiteboard and writes **VISITOR**.
- **Want and process:** Sofia has agreed only to drive Mel home, not to enter orientation. Through the open door she sees baseline observations, proactive risk disclosure, low lighting, a familiar anchor, and a Joined clinician asking assent before each step.
- **Turn / choice:** A Stage 2 visitor leaves after refusing deliberate exposure. Staff provide ordinary care and do not follow. Later the visitor voluntarily returns because the network's absence feels like pain.
- **Residue:** The peaceful month is competent, persuasive, and non-coercive without resolving whether post-exposure choice is free.
- **Last image / next cause:** Sofia stays outside the red floor line while Mel adds a fresh no-contact notation to her record.

### 2. Zero deaths

- **POV / alignment:** Mara.
- **Place / time:** Safe temporary office, same morning.
- **First image:** A world map shows thousands of completed convergences beside a death column containing a single zero.
- **Want and process:** Mara assembles public-health evidence from voluntary examinations, unexplained synchrony, and traceable travel without claiming conventional illness.
- **Turn / choice:** Authorities reject emergency quarantine because the fully Joined are healthy, calm, cooperative, and not legally detainable for sharing information.
- **Residue:** The network's clinical success protects it from the systems designed to detect harm; Mara's warnings begin to look like obsession.
- **Last image / next cause:** She removes Tom from her call list to keep investigators from reaching her through him.

### 3. The notes become a clinic

- **POV / alignment:** Mara observing orientation records.
- **Place / time:** Public-health records room, later morning.
- **First image:** Arlo's original handwritten instruction—**cleaning supplies outside reach**—appears verbatim in clinic checklists from three countries.
- **Want and process:** Mara traces how the first two-host observations matured into standard orientation practice: quiet room, verifiable facts, ordinary hazards removed.
- **Turn / choice:** The identical language proves coordination but also proves that Jenn's delayed report now saves progressing hosts from injury.
- **Residue:** Institutional residue from Part Two becomes both evidence against the network and evidence of its care.
- **Last image / next cause:** A military wellness report arrives using the same uncommon phrasing.

### 4. Six months of inventory

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Military biosurveillance office, late morning.
- **First image:** Six months of unresolved inventory discrepancies vanish from a logistics dashboard overnight.
- **Want and process:** Rios compares small anomalies across commands: identical wellness language, silent duty exchanges, and suddenly perfect inventory.
- **Turn / choice:** Each local explanation is benign; their timing is not. She opens an unofficial cross-site correlation before the reporting threshold is met.
- **Residue:** Rios's competence creates the first institutional view large enough to see the hidden network.
- **Last image / next cause:** Three distant personnel reports align at 78-second intervals on her screen.

### 5. The crane log

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Secure call with tower-crane operator Tess Arnold, afternoon.
- **First image:** Tess's handwritten lift timestamps are more precise than the military system receiving them.
- **Want and process:** Rios needs an independent civilian marker. Tess explains that substitute riggers completed an altered sequence nobody briefed them on, yet every step was safe.
- **Turn / choice:** Tess refuses Rios's invitation to dramatize the event; she reports competent coordination and documents the shutdown sequence she would use if her own motor control changed.
- **Residue:** Tess's safety process and distinctive timestamps enter the evidence chain before their finale payoff.
- **Last image / next cause:** Rios pins the civilian log beside the military correlations and sees the same temporal spacing.

### 6. A plan hidden in maintenance

- **POV / alignment:** Omar Haddad, uninfected.
- **Place / time:** Airport maintenance office and service corridor, afternoon.
- **First image:** Several harmless work requests overlap on a terminal map when Omar holds the pages to the light.
- **Want and process:** Omar wants to stop an unreviewed coordinated indoor-air change. He traces each request to an authorized employee and refuses to infer motive beyond the pattern.
- **Turn / choice:** During inspection he encounters an existing accidental exposure condition and follows safety procedure, but only after a meaningful exposure has occurred. No modifications, calculations, quantities, or apparatus are described.
- **Residue:** Omar reports both the coordinated plan and his exposure as accidental; his maintenance knowledge becomes a future resource and cost.
- **Last image / next cause:** His incident call routes to Rios because his timestamp matches Tess's pattern.

### 7. Tom chooses an answer

- **POV / alignment:** Tom, uninfected.
- **Place / time:** Orientation centre, evening.
- **First image:** Tom sets his wedding ring beside an intake form that asks who must not be contacted.
- **Want and process:** He wants a life without classified absences. Joined staff disclose route, stages, medical risks, altered-consent concerns, and their obligation to wait if he refuses.
- **Turn / choice:** They do not ask where Mara is. Tom actively assents because transparent shared memory looks like care compared with a marriage organized around secrecy.
- **Residue:** His ordinary knowledge of Mara's habits will enter the network after convergence; no one engineers that knowledge for a present attack.
- **Last image / next cause:** He writes Mara's name under **DO NOT CONTACT** before accepting exposure.

### 8. The shielded airman

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Military medical unit, night.
- **First image:** A Stage 3 airman sits inside a radio-shielded room while his EEG still aligns with a distant host.
- **Want and process:** Rios needs to know whether he was recruited, compromised, or controlled. Reconstruction shows prolonged shared quarters with a presymptomatic bunkmate: accidental exposure, not orientation.
- **Turn / choice:** The airman asks to answer without attending to the network. Physicians use short-acting anesthesia under controlled supervision and question him during confused emergence, when access is diminished but not erased.
- **Residue:** He says he did not consent to exposure and still intends to converge because returning to isolation now feels like blindness.
- **Last image / next cause:** His anger fades as network access returns, proving altered regulation without proving falsehood.

### 9. A fact with a source

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Same medical unit, continuous.
- **First image:** Rios places a sealed classified prompt beyond the airman's sight.
- **Want and process:** She tests capability and provenance. With access restored, he answers through connected personnel whose clearances she verifies one by one.
- **Turn / choice:** He says Rios has not spoken to Mateo in nine days, then identifies the source when challenged: a Joined administrative aide saw unanswered reminders on her briefing calendar.
- **Residue:** The reach is invasive but traceable; no mind reading, wall vision, or omniscience is required.
- **Last image / next cause:** Nine red reminder icons glow on Rios's unlocked calendar.

### 10. Prevention is a window

- **POV / alignment:** Mara.
- **Place / time:** Temporary lab and secure call to Rios, next morning.
- **First image:** A small stock ledger sits beside results showing suppression only before widespread interface formation.
- **Want and process:** Mara presents further antisense observations at a high level: the early-treatment window is narrow, the supply is very limited, and established complexes persist.
- **Turn / choice:** Rios asks whether it can disconnect the airman. Mara says no and refuses to dress prevention as a cure.
- **Residue:** Rios hears evidence of an advancing biological state; Mara sees that a scarce intervention could become a tool of forced triage.
- **Last image / next cause:** Rios writes **CONTAIN BEFORE STAGE 3** beneath a result Mara meant as a caution.

### 11. The family definition

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Leah's kitchen, next evening.
- **First image:** Mateo's unanswered messages are displayed on Rios's phone beside the aide's source statement.
- **Want and process:** Rios wants Leah's clinical observations and Mateo's agreement that the network is hostile. Leah reports that Joined patients refuse medication, report no distress, and help staff without demands.
- **Turn / choice:** Mateo accuses Rios of wanting hostility because hostility makes her useful. Leah insists wellness is not the same as obedience; Rios insists calm is not consent.
- **Residue:** Each protects Mateo in a professional language the others experience as accusation. No reconciliation resets the debt.
- **Last image / next cause:** Mateo declines Rios's offered ride and walks home with Leah.

### 12. The doctrine she wrote

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Containment briefing room, final morning.
- **First image:** An old exercise report bears Rios's handwritten correction: **ISOLATE ALL NODES SIMULTANEOUSLY**.
- **Want and process:** She translates the anomaly map into a containment plan with medical teams, communication isolation, synchronized timing, and restoration criteria.
- **Turn / choice:** A colleague recalls why she wrote the doctrine: staggered confirmation once let a simulated threat migrate between facilities. Waiting failed then.
- **Plausible exit:** The government can continue covert observation, public-health engagement, and individual clinical monitoring while the network remains nonlethal.
- **Residue:** Rios's history makes simultaneous action personally and professionally defensible.
- **Last image / next cause:** She carries her old failure report into the authorization meeting.

### 13. He is thanked and ignored

- **POV / alignment:** Rios, with Davis Taffler as the dissenting institutional voice.
- **Place / time:** Interagency authorization room, afternoon.
- **First image:** Davis's agriculture map shows food, transport, utilities, and laboratories beneath the same proposed shutdown overlay.
- **Want and process:** Rios presents a biological adversarial-network classification and requests simultaneous containment authority. Davis traces how isolating every connected system could itself interrupt care and distribution.
- **Turn / choice:** Asked for an alternative, Davis recommends staged engagement with independent medical observation. Rios argues that the network learns from every delay and that staged action repeats her exercise failure.
- **Choice under pressure:** She wins authority and chooses her doctrine despite the peaceful month's zero deaths.
- **Residue / recognition:** Success makes the state capable of acting—and teaches the network that secrecy cannot protect it.
- **Last image / next cause:** Davis is formally thanked; his warning remains on a muted slide while raid clocks synchronize.

### 14. They found us

- **POV / alignment:** Rios outside; Joined people shown without private interiority.
- **Place / time:** Dozens of raid locations, predawn.
- **First image:** Tactical teams wait behind ordinary clinic, warehouse, barracks, and apartment doors.
- **Want and process:** Rios verifies every site and delays the operation once to prevent a medical team from arriving late. The containment machine becomes competent and complete.
- **Turn / choice:** At the final synchronized check, Joined bodies in every location stop their separate tasks and look up simultaneously.
- **Immediate success:** Rios has correctly identified the network and positioned every known node for isolation.
- **Residue / recognition:** The visible coordination confirms her diagnosis while proving the targets know containment is coming.
- **Final image:** Jenn looks toward a window she cannot see through and says quietly, "They found us."

## Episode gate check

- **Two visible exits closed:** Civilian authorities decline conventional quarantine; Rios rejects staged engagement after Davis presents it.
- **Legible procedure:** Pattern correlation, knowledge-source verification, controlled anesthesia limits, and simultaneous-containment planning.
- **Choice creates turn:** Rios applies the doctrine born from her earlier failure and creates the pressure that changes the network's policy.
- **Continuing residue:** Sofia's no-contact record; Arlo's clinic notes; Tom's domestic knowledge; Tess's shutdown procedure; Omar's accidental exposure and maintenance expertise; Mateo's challenge; the narrow antisense window.
- **Rules audit:** First month remains deathless; peaceful exposures require assent; airman and Omar are explicitly accidental; shielding fails; all surprising facts have traceable sources; no Joined private viewpoint.
- **Success and loss in final image:** Rios proves there is a coordinated threat at the instant her competent response makes peaceful concealment impossible.
