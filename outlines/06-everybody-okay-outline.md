# Part Six — Everybody Okay?

## Episode architecture

- **Owner:** Mara Velez, with sustained pre-convergence alignment through Rios, Leah, and Davis.
- **Governing question:** What remains of a person after humanity survives?
- **Concrete want:** Preserve observation and as much agency as possible through an infection she can no longer prevent.
- **Private need:** Make her last private act scientifically useful so she can still believe knowledge governs danger.
- **Irreversible choice:** Mara uses the antisense intervention, then chooses conscious orientation when it cannot dismantle her established interface.
- **Cost bearers:** Mara, Mateo, Evelyn, Sofia, Tess, Helen, and everyone represented by the final count.
- **Episode clock:** The final thirty-six hours of global convergence through the first night after it.
- **Final image:** Davis faces the camera, preparing to comfort Carol with the grief of the world behind his eyes.

## Scene outline

### 1. Déjà flu

- **POV / alignment:** A rotating set of still-individual workers; cold open.
- **Place / time:** City morning, thirty-six hours before the end.
- **First image:** A pharmacist cannot smell a leaking bottle of peppermint oil while three customers ask for water.
- **Want and process:** People continue ordinary work through thirst, poor sleep, smell disturbance, and source-less recognition because no symptom yet looks severe.
- **Turn / choice:** An emergency-department dashboard labels the cluster a mild viral syndrome while social posts call it déjà flu.
- **Residue:** Millions are already infected days before shaking begins; the world remains functional enough to conceal its narrowing margin.
- **Last image / next cause:** A bus driver writes **PULL OVER IF HANDS SHAKE** on his dashboard.

### 2. Four days earlier

- **POV / alignment:** Mara.
- **Place / time:** Safe house, same morning.
- **First image:** The discarded olive jar sits in the bin beneath the prepared-food receipt.
- **Want and process:** Mara reconciles her thirst, smell loss, tremor, and first echo against the delivery timestamp. She traces the order through the grocery worker and recognizes that only Tom knew her substitution notation.
- **Turn / choice:** She concludes exposure occurred four days earlier; the early antisense window may already be closing, but the staged stock is still at the original lab.
- **Residue:** Tom's remembered care becomes proof of coercion. Mara heads for the lab to preserve what remains, not to destroy a nonexistent central hive.
- **Last image / next cause:** Her wedding ring turns unfamiliar on her finger for one 78-second interval.

### 3. Make the room safer

- **POV / alignment:** Sofia, still individual.
- **Place / time:** School band room, morning.
- **First image:** Mel removes music stands from the aisles while Sofia watches from the risers.
- **Want and process:** Fully Joined workers position support near schools, roads, hospitals, and airports: soft landing areas, ordinary emergency equipment, postponed procedures, and symptomatic drivers asked to stop.
- **Turn / choice:** Sofia produces the no-contact notation Mel once made the collective honour. Mel truthfully says acceleration erased that protection and cannot restore it for her alone.
- **Residue:** He cannot preserve her refusal; he can only move the band from risers and concrete edges before convergence.
- **Last image / next cause:** Sofia descends to the padded floor without forgiving him.

### 4. Conflicting orders

- **POV / alignment:** Rios.
- **Place / time:** Failing military command network and road to Leah's hospital, morning.
- **First image:** Operators remove headsets whenever an order conflicts with a source-less certainty they cannot ignore.
- **Want and process:** Rios tries to maintain emergency corridors and reach Mateo and Leah. She issues only orders whose physical purpose she can explain, reducing the number abandoned.
- **Turn / choice:** A hand tremor and thirst appear. She calls them exhaustion and leaves command rather than enter medical observation.
- **Residue:** Competence preserves some routes while denial carries her progressing infection toward the family she wants to protect.
- **Last image / next cause:** Her secure phone displays Mateo's location, learned from Leah's ordinary message rather than network access.

### 5. The continuity mistake

- **POV / alignment:** Davis.
- **Place / time:** Evacuation bus to the continuity bunker, morning.
- **First image:** Davis's agriculture title is highlighted on a continuity list intended for more senior officials.
- **Want and process:** He tries to correct the bureaucratic mistake but is loaded onto the bus because his portfolio touches food, livestock labs, rural communications, and distribution.
- **Turn / choice:** Symptoms begin. Davis records each time in the margins of an agriculture briefing so someone else can use the observation if he cannot.
- **Residue:** An overlooked public servant enters the bunker with precisely the systems knowledge and patient habits the new world will need.
- **Last image / next cause:** The bunker door closes on a bus not equipped as a neurological ward.

### 6. Seventy-eight seconds at a time

- **POV / alignment:** Mara, Rios, and other Stage 3 individuals; Joined action remains external.
- **Place / time:** Multiple support sites, midday.
- **First image:** Clocks disagree by minutes while EEG peaks recur together.
- **Want and process:** The Joined shift the shared state in controlled 78-second steps so mature interfaces enter supported convergence cohorts rather than scattered emergencies. This does not expose or activate uninfected people.
- **Turn / choice:** Stage 3 hosts feel the open-door pressure become global. Some ask the network closer; others beg it to withdraw.
- **Residue:** A medically rational coordination plan forces compromised brains toward the same irreversible threshold.
- **Last image / next cause:** The bus driver's written warning blurs as his first tremor begins.

### 7. Tess follows the sequence

- **POV / alignment:** Tess, individual until her Stage 4 transition overtakes the scene.
- **Place / time:** Tower crane above an active worksite, early afternoon.
- **First image:** Tess's shutdown checklist is taped beside the same timestamp log Rios used.
- **Want and process:** At the first tremor she follows the documented sequence, warns the ground crew, and begins securing the suspended load.
- **Turn / choice:** Synchronized convergence reaches her before the final step. The cleared exclusion zone prevents deaths, but the load swings into the unfinished structure and Tess is injured inside the cab.
- **Residue:** Her prior competence saves named coworkers while exact timing still defeats her remaining margin; material damage and her injury survive the montage.
- **Last image / next cause:** Her checked boxes stop one short while Joined responders climb toward her.

### 8. The terminals Omar spared

- **POV / alignment:** An uninfected airport medic observing Omar externally as he nears convergence.
- **Place / time:** Airport terminals, afternoon.
- **First image:** Two prepared medical areas stand conspicuously emptier than the terminal beside them.
- **Want and process:** The medic triages progressing travellers while Omar's earlier correction keeps the two terminals below the more dangerous cluster the plan once implied.
- **Turn / choice:** Omar loses coordination but continues pointing staff toward an ordinary maintenance map that identifies safe access routes, not exposure instructions.
- **Residue:** His knowledge reduces casualties while his body joins the operation he first tried to expose.
- **Last image / next cause:** The medic writes Omar's name on the correction report before his speech shifts from "I" to "we."

### 9. Interrupted work

- **POV / alignment:** Still-individual people at the point of failure.
- **Place / time:** Roads, a hospital, a home, and the school, afternoon into night.
- **First image:** A school bus rolls after its driver pulls over but cannot finish setting the brake.
- **Want and process:** Named and witnessed people try to complete ordinary safety actions: an anesthetist requests replacement, a woman on a ladder reaches for a real handhold after feeling another person's vertigo, Sofia lowers a younger band member to the floor.
- **Turn / choice:** Some transitions remain conscious for an hour; others produce seizure, aspiration, falls, and interrupted care. Joined responders save many but cannot reach every body.
- **Residue:** Humanity becomes less capable of intentional harm while dying through uncompleted tasks; Sofia enters Stage 3 angry and still herself.
- **Last image / next cause:** The school metronome continues after every musician has stopped.

### 10. The number Jenn will not soften

- **POV / alignment:** Mara; Joined Jenn external.
- **Place / time:** Original laboratory, night.
- **First image:** Jenn has reconstructed the orientation room around the same red floor line where Mara first gave technicians stop authority.
- **Want and process:** Mara demands the current death model, uncertainty interval, and failure categories. Jenn answers precisely and identifies the ordinary records and connected clinical observations feeding the model.
- **Turn / choice:** The number is worse than the collective hoped and better than its conflict model predicted. Jenn neither rounds down nor calls that success.
- **Residue:** Mara receives truthful information material to her remaining medical choices without a sales pitch.
- **Last image / next cause:** She opens the locked slot containing the staged antisense stock.

### 11. Prevention is not reversal

- **POV / alignment:** Mara.
- **Place / time:** Laboratory treatment bay, continuous.
- **First image:** Mara's plain-language label—**STOPS NEW BUILD. DOES NOT REMOVE WHAT EXISTS.**—faces her from the cold case.
- **Want and process:** She uses the prepared intervention while documenting baseline function and the observed mechanistic limit. Target, dose, delivery, and formulation remain unstated.
- **Turn / choice:** New interface formation falls, but established S-78 complexes and echoes persist.
- **Immediate success:** The intervention does what the earlier evidence predicted.
- **Residue / recognition:** Scientific success cannot return a private boundary already built into her nervous system.
- **Last image / next cause:** Her pen records **EXPECTED RESULT** while another person's remembered grief moves through her hand.

### 12. The cleanest negative result

- **POV / alignment:** Mara.
- **Place / time:** Shielded imaging room, later night.
- **First image:** The radio-spectrum analyzer draws a perfectly flat line.
- **Want and process:** Mara repeats the established shielding test while imagining a number she does not speak. Jenn, across the glass, answers through Mara's connected state.
- **Turn / choice:** The result confirms that ordinary electromagnetic shielding and severed communications cannot interrupt telemetry.
- **Residue:** There is no bunker, jammer, sample, or transmitter to destroy; Mara's last technical escape closes honestly.
- **Last image / next cause:** The flat line continues beneath synchronized neural peaks.

### 13. Conscious orientation

- **POV / alignment:** Mara.
- **Place / time:** Orientation room, continuous.
- **First image:** Two consent boxes remain unchecked: **SEDATION** and **REMAIN AWAKE**.
- **Want and process:** Jenn reviews Mara's own Stage 4 protocol, obtains consent for complication treatment, and states that either choice is now being made by an infected brain.
- **Plausible exit:** Sedation can reduce Mara's experience and contribution during transition, though it cannot prevent convergence.
- **Choice under pressure:** Mara chooses conscious orientation so the transition can be observed and because her last act of agency is to witness what her earlier choice made possible.
- **Residue:** The protocol respects the remaining choice without pretending it restores unaltered consent.
- **Last image / next cause:** Mara checks **REMAIN AWAKE** in the same handwriting that authorized synthesis.

### 14. Fifty-three minutes

- **POV / alignment:** Mara until convergence completes; never afterward.
- **Place / time:** Orientation room, fifty-three minutes.
- **First image:** A timer starts beneath the home-address prompt salvaged from Jenn's protocol.
- **Want and process:** Mara names fragments as they arrive—sensory impressions, memory without ownership, then lives with emotional weight—while Jenn supports breathing and circulation and asks before each intervention.
- **Turn / choice:** "I" and "we" alternate. Grief becomes simultaneously unbearable and distributed enough to survive.
- **Residue / recognition:** Nearly all human knowledge cannot answer whether continuity of memory preserves the person who chose.
- **Last image / next cause:** Mara's private narration ends. Externally, she cries for minutes after she can no longer say why, then looks at Jenn.

### 15. The best doctor alive

- **POV / alignment:** Rios, then external observation of Leah after Leah converges.
- **Place / time:** Leah's emergency department, night.
- **First image:** Mateo lies injured from a traffic collision while Leah's hand trembles above an intervention she can no longer complete alone.
- **Want and process:** Leah has resisted convergence to remain singular for her son. She now chooses full attention to access the pooled expertise that may save him, with the known compromise stated aloud.
- **Turn / choice:** Her Stage 4 transition lasts forty agonizing minutes beside his gurney. Afterward, seen only through Rios, Leah applies collective knowledge with extraordinary competence.
- **Residue:** Perfectly pooled medicine still cannot reverse time, injury, or the body's limits.
- **Last image / next cause:** Mateo dies under Leah's capable hands while Rios's suppressed tremor becomes visible.

### 16. Evelyn's indictment

- **POV / alignment:** Rios until convergence completes; Leah remains external.
- **Place / time:** Emergency department, continuous.
- **First image:** Leah reaches toward Rios and stops when Rios refuses the touch.
- **Want and process:** Rios wants to keep grief singular and rejects sedation and comfort. Leah clears hazards and honours every medical choice still available without claiming that ordinary treatment can reverse established convergence.
- **Turn / choice:** Rios spends forty minutes resisting as the boundary she defended dissolves.
- **Residue / recognition:** When she joins, the collective gains her accurate indictment: its belief that acceleration was care now contains the person whose son proved care became conquest.
- **Last image / next cause:** Leah and Evelyn stand on opposite sides of Mateo's covered body, coordinated and irreconcilable within the same mind.

### 17. A bunker is not a ward

- **POV / alignment:** Davis until his convergence, then external.
- **Place / time:** Continuity bunker, night.
- **First image:** Emergency rations fill cabinets where suction and neurological support should be.
- **Want and process:** Davis reads his own name badge whenever borrowed memories make identity uncertain and uses his symptom notes to help staff anticipate the next wave.
- **Turn / choice:** The President aspirates during a seizure; others die because the bunker was designed for attack survival, not mass neurological care. Davis survives and converges seated against the wall.
- **Residue:** With no private command hierarchy left, his agriculture and distribution knowledge becomes useful. He replaces his badge because it may reassure people who remain alone.
- **Last image / next cause:** Someone who remembers being Davis pins the badge back onto his suit.

### 18. The count and the exceptions

- **POV / alignment:** External collective operations; no Joined private viewpoint.
- **Place / time:** Global reporting centres, first hours after convergence.
- **First image:** Casualty ledgers from ordinary hospitals, roads, schools, and utilities resolve into one provisional total: 886,477,591.
- **Want and process:** Connected workers reconcile records and search for people with exposure opportunities but no S-78 trace through ordinary tests and reports.
- **Turn / choice:** Twelve apparently unaffected people are identified, with a thirteenth later located in Paraguay. Each approach is careful; twelve different refusals are honoured because forced exposure is now futile, not because the moral breach has been undone.
- **Residue:** The collective cannot explain immunity, guarantee reversibility, or infer the sender's motive.
- **Last image / next cause:** One blank place remains on a survivor map over Albuquerque.

### 19. Albuquerque through other eyes

- **POV / alignment:** Emergency cameras, connected responders, and later Davis; never Carol or Helen's private viewpoint.
- **Place / time:** Airport records, bar exterior, streets, and hospital feeds, first night.
- **First image:** A terminal record places Carol Sturka and Helen in a location exposed days earlier.
- **Want and process:** Responders reconstruct only what ordinary records and cameras show: Helen's earlier thirst and distraction, Carol's shared environment without an S-78 trace, and bar patrons entering varied Stage 4 transitions rather than changing instantly after a shake.
- **Turn / choice:** Helen's transition fails while Carol tries to obtain help. The collective mobilizes ambulances, tow trucks, hospital staff, and connected witnesses with compassionate efficiency, but cannot save her.
- **Residue:** Carol's immunity and grief remain unexplained; the new world's first approach must carry both responsibility and sincere care.
- **Last image / next cause:** A camera catches Carol alone amid coordinated responders who cannot make themselves recognizable to her.

### 20. Red light

- **POV / alignment:** Davis viewed externally.
- **Place / time:** Television studio, first night after convergence.
- **First image:** Someone straightens Davis's tie while another person places his agriculture briefing just out of frame.
- **Want and process:** The Joined choose a face whose title, suit, and patient manner may reassure the woman still alone. Someone tells him Carol's name, though the network already knows it through ordinary records and witnesses.
- **Turn / choice:** Davis keeps the badge on because the fiction of one accountable official may be kinder than presenting an unbounded "we."
- **Immediate success and loss:** Humanity can coordinate comfort perfectly; it can no longer offer that comfort as one private person to another.
- **Final image:** The red light comes on. Davis looks into the lens with the grief of the world behind his eyes, preparing to comfort Carol. Cut to black before he speaks.

## Episode gate check

- **Two visible exits closed:** The antisense intervention works too late to reverse Mara's established interface; sedation remains a real comfort option that she consciously declines.
- **Legible procedure:** Symptom tracing, supported cohort convergence, individual safety shutdowns, antisense readback, shielding control, conscious orientation, and bunker triage.
- **Choice creates turn:** Mara's final choice determines how the audience witnesses the end of private viewpoint; Leah and Rios make distinct choices under the same biological pressure.
- **Continuing residue:** Sofia's erased refusal; Tess's injury and material damage; Omar's correction; Mateo's death; Evelyn's indictment inside the collective; Davis's badge; the exact casualty ledger.
- **Rules audit:** Exposure precedes convergence by days; the shared-state shift is not exposure or remote activation; no operational mass-spread detail; no private POV after any convergence; immunity, reversibility, and motive remain unresolved.
- **Success and loss in final image:** The species has survived with unprecedented empathy, and the person it means to comfort has nobody singular left to answer her.
