7. Mara’s antisense result preserves a prevention path while removing the false promise of reversal for established interfaces.
8. Arlo’s clinically required sedation demonstrates dependence on active brain function without staging a dangerous experiment for that purpose.
9. A connected specialist’s learned pattern prompts Nia’s timely ordinary care, giving the network its first concrete institutional benefit.
10. Jenn’s forty-minute convergence provides a truthful orientation that makes Deshpande’s second exit more informed, not less available.
11. Deshpande chooses conscious progression and loses the private boundary on which his authority depended.
12. The saved colleague and the Joined pair’s conduct make voluntary recruitment emotionally credible to Mel.
13. Mel protects Sofia on paper, accepts that the protection is not absolute, and chooses exposure for himself.

**Result:** Evidence does not force the moral turns. Deshpande and Mel each close a plausible exit for understandable but contestable reasons.

## Greenlight answers and watchpoints

- **Potential forcing:** Mel has a waiting interval, an unexposed alternative caregiver, employment protection, and an open door. He chooses after the network’s benefit and uncertainty are both made concrete.
- **Potential intelligence failure:** The anesthesia observation piggybacks on clinically necessary imaging; care authority remains with the anesthesiologist. Nia receives ordinary emergency evaluation rather than remote treatment by intuition.
- **Removable-scene test:** The photo proves the access route; shielding forces the model; antisense plants the finale limit; anesthesia preserves the brain-as-processor rule; Nia makes recruitment credible; both convergence scenes create the consent problem.
- **Long consequence:** Orientation language, the prevention/reversal distinction, failed shielding, the cure myth, Arlo’s remote notes, Sofia’s exclusion, and Mel’s assent all remain active beyond the episode.
- **Memorable images:** Two clocks required to disagree; an unpublished lime photograph; expensive rooms that do nothing; **PREVENTION IS NOT REVERSAL**; an address taped to the ceiling; a pen stopped halfway through **I**; a blue name label pressed down before the bag opens.
- **Hidden suffering:** The episode can make Deshpande’s chosen convergence feel cleaner than Jenn’s accidental exposure. Later parts must preserve that Jenn’s orientation competence does not retroactively authorize what happened to her or Arlo.
- **Drafting watchpoint:** Part Four should not repeat the laboratory proof suite. It should spend detail on how evidence, care, persuasion, and institutional learning behave outside this controlled setting.
