# Part Two — Forty-Seven Hours

## Episode architecture

- **Owner:** Jenn
- **Governing question:** Has anything happened?
- **Concrete want:** Remain a scientific actor while determining whether her symptoms and Arlo's impossible correlation are real.
- **Private need:** Obtain proof that she is neither damaged nor disposable before the institution can define her as evidence.
- **Irreversible choice:** Jenn coaches Arlo through their first stable echo and delays reporting the correlation that would separate them.
- **Cost bearer:** Arlo, and the integrity of containment.
- **Episode clock:** 3:36 a.m. through hour forty-seven after Jenn's bite.
- **Final image:** Jenn and Arlo sit upright on separate floors as their monitors settle into the same rhythm.

## Scene outline

### 1. Eight months of bupkis

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn; cold open.
- **Place / time:** Animal facility, 3:08 a.m., eight months after synthesis.
- **First image:** Species names are crossed off a whiteboard beneath a funding-review date.
- **Want and process:** Jenn completes the overnight rounds across macaques, rabbits, mice, guinea pigs, and rats, recording the same absence of clinical signs with exacting care.
- **Turn / choice:** Upstairs, a freezer-space dispute arrives marked urgent while her clean observations are treated as nothing. She signs anyway rather than embellish a result.
- **Residue:** The project's grand promise has become routine labour; Jenn's need for recognition is attached to meticulous evidence.
- **Last image / next cause:** One rat's status light remains on after its cage card says it is due for euthanasia.

### 2. Doughnuts at the checkpoint

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Security desk outside the animal corridor, 3:19 a.m.
- **First image:** Two boxes of slowly staling doughnuts sit beside the sign-in sheet under a hand-drawn trombone.
- **Want and process:** Mel Ortega tries to sell the night shift fundraiser doughnuts for his fourteen-year-old daughter Sofia's marching band. Jenn wants coffee and five quiet minutes before the final rat check.
- **Turn / choice:** Mel admits he is anxious about a security review, not the doughnuts. Jenn buys one and leaves it unopened when the animal-room alarm chirps.
- **Residue:** Mel, Sofia, the fundraiser label, and his panic are planted before the object becomes consequential.
- **Last image / next cause:** The doughnut goes stale beside Jenn's name while she badges back through the red floor line.

### 3. 3:36 a.m.

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Rat procedure room, 3:36 a.m.
- **First image:** A motionless rat lies under a clear cover while the monitor shows a heartbeat too forceful for its body.
- **Want and process:** Jenn follows the established check before euthanasia, verifying the animal identifier, monitor lead, and apparent unresponsiveness.
- **Turn / choice:** The rat surges and bites through her glove. Jenn secures the animal before reaching for her own hand, preserving containment at personal cost.
- **Residue:** Meaningful biological exposure by bite; damaged glove, wound, timestamp, and incident alarm.
- **Last image / next cause:** Blood beads inside the glove as the wall clock changes from 3:36 to 3:37.

### 4. The emergency protocol

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Decontamination threshold and observation bay, minutes later.
- **First image:** Deshpande reads a laminated checklist while Jenn's bitten hand shakes above the first unchecked box.
- **Want and process:** The team secures the animal, isolates Jenn, documents the route, cleans the room, and preserves samples under the protocol rehearsed in Part One.
- **Turn / choice:** Jenn suffers an acute autonomic event—whole-body tremor, gasping, loss of muscle control—but does not converge or become placid. Deshpande treats the symptoms rather than assume a mechanism.
- **Residue:** A visible event serious enough to trigger observation but too nonspecific to prove infection.
- **Last image / next cause:** Jenn stabilizes as the cleanup continues without her supervision.

### 5. The cut nobody classifies correctly

- **POV / alignment:** Arlo briefly, preserving Jenn as owner.
- **Place / time:** Procedure-room cleanup, approximately four hours after Jenn's bite.
- **First image:** Arlo pulls a fresh bandage tight over a cut on his hand before putting on his cleaning glove.
- **Want and process:** Facilities must return the room to containment status. Arlo follows the cleanup handoff while staff still assume Jenn cannot transmit anything onward.
- **Turn / choice:** Contaminated material from Jenn reaches the broken skin when a disposal boundary is reset out of sequence. Arlo reports the sting and shows the cut.
- **Residue:** The event is documented as a housekeeping injury, explicitly not recognized as biological exposure. Brief touch and intact skin play no role.
- **Last image / next cause:** Arlo's incident form enters the facilities system, separate from Jenn's biological record.

### 6. Potential

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Negative-pressure observation room, hour two.
- **First image:** An occupational-health physician writes **POTENTIAL EXPOSURE** beneath a photograph of the bite.
- **Want and process:** Jenn wants her firsthand account included in the record and asks to participate in the sampling schedule. The physician wants a compliant patient and a clean chain of custody.
- **Turn / choice:** Jenn bargains for access to her own time-stamped observations in exchange for remaining in isolation without protest.
- **Residue:** She retains limited scientific agency, but the institution now controls her room, samples, and movements.
- **Last image / next cause:** The observation window becomes a mirror when the corridor lights switch off.

### 7. Twelve normal hours

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Observation room and adjacent lab, hours three through twelve.
- **First image:** A grid of normal results fills Mara's screen while Jenn repeats the same cognitive card set behind glass.
- **Want and process:** Mara, Deshpande, and Jenn establish a baseline and watch for a deviation; each procedure's goal and stop condition is clear.
- **Turn / choice:** Nothing deviates. Deshpande argues for transfer to ordinary occupational observation; Mara keeps the stricter room until a second independent set returns.
- **Plausible exit:** If the next results remain normal, Jenn can leave and the event can close as an animal bite.
- **Residue:** Competence buys time without proving danger; Jenn feels the project preparing to make her irrelevant again.
- **Last image / next cause:** Coffee arrives at hour nineteen and smells like hot water.

### 8. The first missing sense

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Observation room, hours nineteen through twenty-six.
- **First image:** Jenn holds coffee under her nose, then checks the cup label as if it might explain the absence.
- **Want and process:** She repeats smell controls without prompting and logs thirst, poor sleep, and a brief shiver rather than hiding them.
- **Turn / choice:** At hour twenty-six she answers Deshpande's unfinished question about the next card. He changes the sequence; she cannot repeat the effect.
- **Residue:** The first Stage 1 signs and one source-less semantic answer are on record, but remain explainable as stress and cueing.
- **Last image / next cause:** Deshpande turns the card face down before either of them names what happened.

### 9. Rat light

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Observation room, hour thirty-one.
- **First image:** Fluorescent bars from the corridor stretch across Jenn's ceiling like the top of a cage.
- **Want and process:** Jenn tries to sleep through the scheduled dark interval.
- **Turn / choice:** She dreams from the rat's sensory position—light, gloved hands, enclosure—without a human concept of skin as a boundary. On waking she records the dream despite knowing how it will sound.
- **Residue:** The sensory echo follows a plausible early pathway and preserves her honesty before later concealment.
- **Last image / next cause:** Her handwritten phrase **not my angle** sits beside the rat's cage number.

### 10. A trace near the bite

- **POV / alignment:** Mara through Jenn's observation-window view.
- **Place / time:** Diagnostic bench and observation room, hour thirty-four.
- **First image:** Mara places two result sheets against the glass: a local trace and a negative blood sample.
- **Want and process:** Mara orders independent confirmation, maps where the trace has and has not appeared, and refuses to call it systemic infection. Deshpande tests contamination explanations.
- **Turn / choice:** Because the finding may represent new complex manufacture, Mara opens an antisense-candidate line while withholding all target design, formulation, and dosing detail.
- **Residue:** The intervention is planted before anyone knows whether it can help; the strict observation order remains in force.
- **Last image / next cause:** A new work-order number appears under Jenn's name while an unrelated facilities alert flashes for Arlo.

### 11. Borrowed panic

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Observation room, hour forty.
- **First image:** Jenn's own pulse is steady while the monitor alarm in her mind seems to accelerate.
- **Want and process:** She tests whether the feeling is hers by inventorying her body, the adjacent rooms, and the timing of staff entries.
- **Turn / choice:** She correctly identifies Mel hiding a panic attack at security, then senses Arlo alone in a supply room staring at the inflamed cut on his knuckle.
- **Residue:** Interoceptive and spatial echoes follow the progressing neural pattern; Jenn now has a second-host hypothesis and a knowledge route she can verify.
- **Last image / next cause:** She asks the nurse to call facilities without explaining why.

### 12. The separated records

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Observation room and supply-room video call, hours forty-one through forty-six.
- **First image:** Jenn's biological incident and Arlo's housekeeping injury appear on different halves of Mara's screen.
- **Want and process:** Mara reconciles timestamps and orders Arlo assessed. Deshpande prepares separate transfers to preserve independent observations if a correlation appears.
- **Turn / choice:** Arlo reports only an inflamed cut and sleeplessness. Jenn sees that one truthful statement from her will trigger deeper isolation before either host can describe the phenomenon.
- **Plausible exit:** She can report the emotional correlation now and let the containment system separate them cleanly.
- **Residue:** The transfer orders wait unsigned while Mara directly asks Jenn whether anything else has changed.
- **Last image / next cause:** The call clock reaches forty-seven hours; Jenn hears recognition arrive before Arlo looks up.

### 13. Attend, then withdraw

- **POV / alignment:** Jenn.
- **Place / time:** Separate observation rooms two floors apart, hour forty-seven.
- **First image:** Arlo grips a supply-room chair while Jenn watches his fear without any camera showing him.
- **Want and process:** Jenn needs to establish whether this is correlation or hallucination and keep Arlo from panicking. She coaches him through the first non-operational attention exercise: notice the foreign impression, verify one ordinary fact, then withdraw.
- **Turn / choice:** Arlo's recognition of Jenn stabilizes both of their breathing. When Mara repeats her question, Jenn withholds the correlation and says she has nothing she can separate from stress yet.
- **Immediate success:** The two-host connection becomes repeatable enough to observe; Arlo is calmer and remains nearby overnight.
- **Residue / recognition:** Jenn has normalized cooperation with the infection and contaminated the integrity of every later observation. Arlo's notes and trust will carry her choice forward.
- **Final image:** Jenn and Arlo sit upright in the dark on separate floors. Their heart monitors settle into the same rhythm, offset by a fraction of a second.

## Episode gate check

- **Two visible exits closed:** Normal results could end strict observation; Jenn could report the first stable correlation and permit immediate separation.
- **Legible procedure:** Bite response, containment cleanup, baseline observation, independent confirmation, and two-record reconciliation.
- **Choice creates turn:** The bite starts the crisis, but Jenn's deliberate delay creates the first stable two-host network.
- **Continuing residue:** Arlo's misclassified injury and notes; Jenn's compromised report; the antisense work order; Mel's panic and doughnuts.
- **Rules audit:** Bite and broken-skin contamination are meaningful exposures; symptoms progress over forty-seven hours; shaking is an acute event, not instant Joining; echoes follow neuroanatomical modes.
- **Success and loss in final image:** Jenn proves she is not hallucinating by creating evidence she has made institutionally unreliable.
