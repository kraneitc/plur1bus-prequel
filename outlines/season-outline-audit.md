## Transmission, progression, and telemetry audit

- Jenn is exposed by the rat bite at exactly 3:36 a.m.; her acute shaking is not convergence.
- Arlo is accidentally exposed roughly four hours later when contaminated material reaches a fresh cut. His first stable echo arrives about forty-three hours after that exposure.
- Deshpande, Mel, and Tom receive proactive material-risk disclosure and actively assent during the peaceful phase. Sofia refuses and remains no-contact for that month.
- Omar and the Stage 3 airman are identified as accidental exposures, not disguised orientation.
- The first month remains nonlethal. Acceleration follows military discovery and the eleven-minute containment success.
- Mass spread uses many distributed ordinary systems and presymptomatic travel. Outlines omit quantities, performance thresholds, preparation, apparatus changes, and optimization.
- The global shared-state change coordinates already mature interfaces; it neither exposes nor remotely activates uninfected people.
- Distance, Faraday shielding, jamming, cut telecommunications, and the bunker fail consistently. Brain state still changes contribution and access.
- No sample, transmitter, command body, or central hive can be destroyed to end the network.
- Early echoes follow sensory, autonomic, mnemonic, then semantic involvement rather than changing arbitrarily.

## Truth and consent audit

- Fully Joined speakers are never used to deliver a knowingly false fact. They answer narrowly only when omitted information is not material to the questioner's consent.
- Jenn discloses uncertainty and the worst part of Stage 4 to Deshpande and Mel; uncertainty strengthens rather than weakens the persuasion.
- During the peaceful month, a refusal produces waiting and ordinary care. The network does not manufacture accidents to evade assent.
- The compromised nature of Stage 2+ assent is stated inside orientation scenes rather than left as subtext.
- Part Five names the change in policy: aggregate-harm reasoning displaces individual assent. The outline does not call this consent.
- After acceleration, Joined care remains sincere and materially effective; sincerity never excuses coercion.

## Safety-abstraction audit

- The outlines retain conceptual biology, broad routes, observed results, timing, symptoms, and moral consequence.
- They omit real sequences, synthesis or cultivation workflow, target design, quantities, dose, culture conditions, environmental thresholds, apparatus modifications, distribution calculations, and formulation.
- The intervention is described as used or administered without specifying its delivery method, following the higher-order project rule even where exploratory treatment wording was more specific.
- Sample construction and contaminated-food preparation occur outside the frame. Mass spread is represented through authorized ordinary acts whose operational combination remains unstated.
